Every workplace has rituals. Ours are documented here in case you accidentally break one and wonder why everyone is looking at you funny.

## The Friday Deploy Rule

<Warning>
  We do not deploy on Fridays. We also do not talk about deploying on Fridays. If you say the word "deploy" on a Friday, you owe the on-call engineer a coffee.
</Warning>

## The Standup Ceremony

<Steps>
  <Step title="Arrive 90 seconds late">
    Arriving on time is considered showing off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say your update in exactly three sentences">
    Four sentences and someone will passive-aggressively mute you next time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="End with 'no blockers'">
    Even if you have blockers. Especially if you have blockers. Raise them in DM afterwards.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The Snack Cabinet Hierarchy

- **Top shelf:** whoever restocked it last week gets naming rights for one item. Current entry: "Debbie's Emotional Support Pretzels." Do not touch Debbie's pretzels.
- **Middle shelf:** free for all.
- **Bottom shelf:** the graveyard. Contains a granola bar from 2021. Nobody eats from the bottom shelf. Nobody throws anything out either. This is the way.

## The Meeting Room Names

Every meeting room is named after a fruit that isn't technically a fruit. Yes, this includes Tomato, Avocado, and Rhubarb. If you book "Strawberry" you will be quietly judged, because strawberries are technically not berries but you'd need to look that up to know why.
