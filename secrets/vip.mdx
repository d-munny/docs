## The secret handshake

<Steps>
  <Step title="Approach with confidence">
    Confidence, not eye contact. Eye contact triggers the sprinklers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say the phrase">
    "The heron flies at midnight, but only on Tuesdays."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the countersign">
    If they respond "Then I hope it packed a jacket," you're in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do NOT improvise">
    Last quarter someone said "The heron flies whenever, honestly" and we had to rebuild the CI pipeline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## VIP code of conduct

<Warning>
  With great access comes great responsibility, and also occasional embarrassment.
</Warning>

1. Do not tell the non-VIPs about the free espresso martinis. There are no free espresso martinis, but if they start asking, we'll have to invent some.
2. Do not use `enable_chaos_mode` in production. Or do. We're not your parents.
3. If you find a bug, name it after a cheese. Tradition.
4. The office cat outranks you. This is not a joke, this is the org chart.
